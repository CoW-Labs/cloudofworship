# NightCard

A `night` card with a lavender title, used to make the mission point inside a purple poster.

**Provide** a `.ds-night-card` with a `.ds-night-card-title`, a paragraph and an uppercase `.ds-night-card-meta` link.

**Colour** title `purple-200` (14:1), body `mist` (15.5:1), link `purple-300` (10.8:1).
**Don't** put a PillButton inside, or use it on white.

**Group:** Cards

## Markup

```html
<div class="ds-night-card"><h3 class="ds-night-card-title">Your plan equips a ministry.</h3><p>When your church subscribes to Teams, you help put the tools for spreading the good news into the hands of churches that need them most.</p><a class="ds-night-card-meta" href="#">See Teams pricing</a></div>
```
