# NumberedPoints

Three principles in a row, each under a hairline with a purple step number.

**Provide** an `<ol class="ds-points">` of three `.ds-point` items: a two-digit `.ds-point-num`, a three-word `.ds-point-title` and one short sentence.

**Use** for reasons or principles that are read in order. Stack to one column under 768px.
**Don't** number things that are not a list, or write more than one sentence per point.

**Group:** Sections

## Markup

```html
<ol class="ds-points">
<li class="ds-point"><span class="ds-point-num">01</span><h3 class="ds-point-title">Nothing hidden</h3><p class="ds-point-body">Read every line of the app your church runs on Sunday.</p></li>
<li class="ds-point"><span class="ds-point-num">02</span><h3 class="ds-point-title">Yours to change</h3><p class="ds-point-body">Fix a bug or build what your church needs.</p></li>
<li class="ds-point"><span class="ds-point-num">03</span><h3 class="ds-point-title">Shaped by churches</h3><p class="ds-point-body">Ideas come from the teams who use it every week.</p></li>
</ol>
```
