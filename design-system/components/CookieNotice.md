# CookieNotice

A small lilac card in the bottom-right corner that explains cookies in one friendly sentence.

**Provide** a `.ds-cookie` dialog with one sentence and a violet PillButton “That's Okay”.

**Voice** say what the cookies do and tie it to Sunday; no legal wording in the card (link the Cookie Notice in the footer).
**Don't** block the page or add an “Accept all / Reject all” matrix here. Gradient values are recreated from a screenshot.

**Group:** Feedback

## Markup

```html
<div class="ds-cookie" role="dialog" aria-label="Cookies"><p>We keep a few cookies around. They help us see what’s working, so this site runs as smoothly as your Sunday service should.</p><button class="ds-pill-btn">That’s Okay</button></div>
```
