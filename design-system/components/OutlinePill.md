# OutlinePill

A 2px outlined pill used as a section tag or anchor link on the purple poster.

**Provide** an `<a class="ds-outline-pill">` with a title-case name of a page section or initiative. `.sm` is the 40px mobile size.

**Use** at the top-right of a poster section to name it or link to its explainer (“Our open source philosophy”, “The Ends of The Earth Initiative”).
**Don't** use it as the main call to action (that is a PillButton) or on light grounds.

**Group:** Actions

## Markup

```html
<div class="row"><a class="ds-outline-pill" href="#">Our open source philosophy</a><a class="ds-outline-pill sm" href="#">The Ends of The Earth Initiative</a></div>
```
