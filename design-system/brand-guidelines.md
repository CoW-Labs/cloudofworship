# Brand guidelines

## Who we are

Cloud of Worship makes church media projection easy. It runs in the browser, keeps working offline, and lets a whole team prepare and run Sunday's slides together. It is free to start, open source under the GNU AGPLv3, and made by CoW Labs.

**Mission.** The gospel is for everyone. The tools that share it should be too.

**Who it's for.** Growing churches of under about 1,000 people, and the volunteers who run their screens: media teams, youth and children's ministry, the pastor's assistant on a Saturday night. Many have small budgets, older laptops and unreliable Wi-Fi. They are in 63+ countries, with deep roots across Africa.

**What we promise.** Your service should not depend on one person who knows how to use the software.

**What we give back.** 90% of profits from Teams plan sales go to pushing the gospel forward in churches across Africa and other regions (the Ends of The Earth Initiative).

## Personality

| We are | So we | We are not |
|---|---|---|
| Servant-hearted | Measure success in calmer Sundays, not features | Preachy or churchy |
| Plain-spoken | Use short words and real numbers | Corporate, salesy or jargon-heavy |
| Warm | Joke gently and admit when we're asleep | Cute or flippant about worship |
| Open | Show the code, the price and the profits | Secretive or vague |
| Communal | Talk about teams, not heroes | About “power users” |

## Naming

- Write **Cloud of Worship** in full, with a capital C and W and a lowercase “of”. Never “CloudOfWorship”, “Cloud Of Worship” or “the Cloud”.
- **COW** is fine in support copy (“Contact COW”); **CoW Labs** is the company in legal lines. Never put either in the logo lockup.
- The paid plan is **Teams** (“the Teams plan”). The free plan is just free.
- Features are named by what a volunteer does: Bible & scripture, Songs & hymns, Live projection, Live transcription, Team Workflow, Offline-first experience, Browser-ready UX.

## Voice

Talk like the experienced volunteer who shows a newcomer the ropes before service: confident, kind and brief.

1. **Lead with Sunday.** Tie benefits to the service and the people running it. “Keeps going when the Wi-Fi doesn't.” “Plan Sunday's service together.”
2. **Say it in under ten words.** Headlines are short sentences in sentence case, often two lines. “Put scripture on screen in seconds.”
3. **Use real numbers.** “3500+ churches served.” “99% uptime during service hours.” “Save at least 10 minutes every Sunday.” Never round up or invent.
4. **Name the worry, then remove it.** “Your sub-1000 church congregation doesn't need overly complex software or overburdened volunteers.” “Monthly cost too high? Tell us what your church can afford.”
5. **Be honest about money.** State the price in full, the discount, the giving pledge, and that free is free: “You can start for free and pay only if your church needs it.”
6. **Allow one smile.** “We'll reply in about 30 minutes—or 8.5 hours if we're asleep :)” One per page is plenty.
7. **Scripture with care.** Quote verses accurately with the reference and translation (“Romans 5:8 NKJV”). Use biblical language for mission statements (“To the ends of the earth”), not for features.

**Grammar.** “We” is Cloud of Worship; “you” and “your church” is the reader. Use contractions. Put the action in buttons as a verb: “Get started for free”, “See the code on GitHub”, “Contact us”.

**Real examples**

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| Revolutionary AI-powered worship presentation platform | Run your church media projection easily |
| Seamless offline synchronization | Keeps going when the Wi-Fi doesn't |
| Advanced fuzzy search | Find any song or hymn, typos and all |
| Real-time multi-user collaboration | Plan Sunday's service together |
| Voice-activated scripture detection | Say the verse, see it on screen |
| Flexible pricing tiers available | You can start for free and pay only if your church needs it |
| We value your privacy | We keep a few cookies around. They help us see what's working, so this site runs as smoothly as your Sunday service should. |

## Logo

The mark is a cloud cut by a cross of negative space: a vertical split down the middle and a horizontal gap near the top. It comes in three inks (see `assets/logos`):

- **Gradient** (`cow-mark.svg`) on white, lavender and black.
- **White** (`cow-mark-white.svg`) on the dark stage and night sections.
- **Ink** (`cow-mark-ink.svg`) on the purple poster.

The lockup sets “Cloud of Worship” in Bricolage Grotesque Bold to the right of the mark, vertically centred, with a gap of about a quarter of the mark's width. Keep clear space of half the mark's width on all sides. Recommended: below about 24px tall, use the mark alone.

**Don't** rotate, outline, stretch, re-gradient or add effects to the mark; place the gradient mark on purple; set the name in another typeface; or lock it up with taglines.

## Putting it together

A typical page, top to bottom: the dark stage hero with projected slides and a violet pill; white sections with product footage in glass windows; a statement in large condensed type surrounded by teammates' cursors; lavender stats and pricing with a ticket and two tilted notes; night testimonials and the church-logo wall; a purple poster that asks “Sunday's coming. Are you ready?”; the black footer.

When in doubt: fewer words, bigger type, one violet pill, and something real from a church's Sunday on screen.
