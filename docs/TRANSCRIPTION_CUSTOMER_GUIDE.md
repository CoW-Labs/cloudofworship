# Sermon transcription and voice controls

Cloud of Worship can listen through your selected microphone, turn speech into a live transcript, find Bible passages, and respond to a small set of spoken controls.

## Before the service

1. Open **Settings > Microphone Settings** and select the microphone you want to use.
2. Open **Settings > Bible Slide Settings** and make sure the Bible translations you want are available on the device.
3. Open **Settings > Other Settings** and review these options:
   - **Automatic transcription actions** allows detected Bible references and voice navigation controls to update the live slide.
   - **Voice Bible version changes** allows spoken controls such as “switch to NIV.”
4. For best results, use a clear microphone, reduce room noise, and keep a reliable internet connection.

## Start and stop transcription

1. Open **Quick Actions** and choose **Transcribe Sermon**.
2. Select the **microphone** button in the Transcribe panel.
3. Speak normally. Words will appear in the **Transcripts** tab.
4. Select the **stop** button when the service is finished.

The moving sound level shows whether Cloud of Worship can hear the microphone. If it does not move while someone is speaking, check the selected microphone and the browser's microphone permission.

## Is there a trigger word?

No. You do not need to say “Hey Cloud of Worship” before a control.

While transcription is running, Cloud of Worship checks everything it hears for supported phrases. Say a control clearly, preferably as a short sentence followed by a brief pause. The spoken control will also remain visible in the transcript.

## Bible references

Simply say a complete Bible reference, for example:

- “John 3:16”
- “John chapter 3 verse 16”
- “Second Corinthians 5:17”
- “Matthew chapter 3 verses 20 to 22”

When **Automatic transcription actions** is on, the first detected reference updates an existing Bible slide or creates one, then takes it live. Other detected or suggested passages appear in the **Scriptures** tab and can be selected manually.

A book or chapter without a verse, such as “John 3,” is not enough. Say the book, chapter, and verse.

## Voice controls

The phrases below are reliable examples. Similar natural wording may also be recognised.

| What you want | Try saying |
| --- | --- |
| Move forward | “Next verse”, “Show the next verse”, “Go forward”, “Continue to the next verse” |
| Move back | “Previous verse”, “Go back one verse”, “Bring it back”, “Return to the previous verse” |
| Go to a numbered verse | “Verse 7”, “Go to verse 12”, “Show verse number twenty-three” |
| Change Bible translation | “Switch to NIV”, “Show this passage in KJV”, “Give me Amplified”, “Use the New King James Version” |
| Move and change translation together | “Next verse in Amplified”, “Go to verse 7 in NIV”, “Previous verse in the New King James Version” |

For the most predictable result during a live service, you can give translation and navigation controls separately. For example, say “Switch to NIV,” wait for the slide to change, then say “Next verse.”

Useful control words include:

- Forward: **next**, **following**, **forward**, **ahead**, **continue**, **advance**, **proceed**
- Back: **previous**, **prev**, **back**, **backward**, **return**, **bring it back**
- Numbered verse: **verse**, **verse number**, often with **go**, **move**, **jump**, **show**, **open**, or **display**
- Translation: **switch**, **change**, **set**, **use**, **show**, **display**, or **give me**

Numbered verse controls accept digits or spoken numbers from 1 to 176. On a Bible slide, the number refers to a verse in the chapter currently on screen. It does not mean a chapter number.

## Bible translation names

You can say the translation's short code or full name, for example **NIV**, **N K J V**, **New King James Version**, **AMP**, or **Amplified Bible**.

Only the default translation and translations already available on the device can be selected. Translation changes work only when the currently selected slide is a Bible slide.

Some translation names are also common sermon words. To prevent unwanted changes, use a clear instruction for these:

- Say “Switch to The Message,” not only “The Message.”
- Say “Give me The Passion Translation,” not only “Passion.”
- Say “Use the World English Bible,” not only “Web.”

Their short codes, **MSG**, **TPT**, and **WEB**, can also be used.

## What currently works

- Live English transcription while the microphone is running
- Automatic detection of complete Bible references, including common spoken book names, spoken numbers, and verse ranges
- Bible passage suggestions based on the latest part of the sermon
- Next, previous, and numbered verse controls for the selected Bible, song, hymn, or song setlist content
- Bible translation changes on the selected Bible slide
- Selecting a detected or suggested passage manually from the Transcripts or Scriptures tab

## What is not currently supported

- A wake phrase or custom trigger word
- Starting, stopping, or clearing transcription by voice
- Commands such as “next chapter,” “go to chapter 4,” or “show the whole chapter”
- Moving through PowerPoint or presentation pages with voice controls
- Opening a song, hymn, media item, schedule, or general slide by name
- Changing a Bible translation that is not available on the device
- Changing translations while a non-Bible slide is selected
- Languages other than English for speech recognition and voice controls
- A built-in button to save, download, copy, or reopen a transcript from the Transcribe panel

The transcript shown in the panel is a working view for the current session. Closing the panel ends the active transcription view, and **Clear transcript** removes the visible text. Do not rely on this panel as the only permanent sermon record.

## Avoiding accidental slide changes

Because there is no wake phrase, ordinary sermon wording can occasionally sound like a control. For example, phrases containing “next verse,” “move forward,” or “bring it back” may update the selected slide.

If you want a transcript and suggestions without automatic live changes, turn off **Settings > Other Settings > Automatic transcription actions**. Bible references will still appear for the operator to select manually.

## Troubleshooting

- **Nothing is being transcribed:** allow microphone access, confirm the correct microphone in Settings, and check that the sound level moves.
- **The microphone will not start:** use an up-to-date Chrome, Edge, or Safari browser and check the internet connection.
- **A control is ignored:** say it as a short, clear phrase, confirm Automatic transcription actions is on, and make sure the correct type of slide is selected.
- **A translation is ignored:** confirm Voice Bible version changes is on, select a Bible slide, and make sure that translation is available on the device.
- **A Bible reference is missed:** say the complete book, chapter, and verse, then pause briefly. For example, say “First Corinthians chapter 13 verse 4.”
- **The weekly timer reaches zero:** online transcription stops when the account's weekly allowance is used. The allowance resets on Monday.
